# Research notes: courier tracking in Pakistan

## SMS gateways
- Gateway A: lower price per message, delivery reports arrive in batches every few minutes.
- Gateway B: higher price, real-time delivery reports, better documentation.
- Both need sender ID registration, which can take one to two weeks.

## Rider interviews (5 riders, Lahore)
- Signal drops in markets and basements, so offline mode is essential.
- Riders prefer large buttons and Urdu labels.
- Cash handover at shift end is the most stressful step; they want a clear running total.

## Competitor scan
- Most local couriers track parcels in spreadsheets and WhatsApp groups.
- Two regional apps offer tracking links, but neither reconciles cash on delivery.
